Validate and preview custom .NET TimeSpan and DateTime format strings for the player timecode display.

Back to Guide
TimeSpan
Valid format
Preview: 01:23:45.678

Playback Duration (TimeSpan)

Used when no date tokens (dd, MM, yy) are present. Literal separators must be escaped with a backslash (\: and \.).

Date & System Clock (DateTime)

Triggered automatically when dd, MM, or yy is included. Backslashes are not needed. Use MM for month and mm for minutes.
