Core Features

Engineered for Playback, DSP & Control

Native WinUI 3 performance combined with hardware-accelerated decoding, multi-endpoint audio routing, and zero telemetry.

Flyleaf & DirectX

Universal Playback Engine

  • No External Codecs Required: Play MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, FLV, MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG, and dozens of other formats out of the box.
  • DirectX Hardware Acceleration: Smooth, low-latency video decoding and presentation powered by Flyleaf and DirectX.
  • Real-Time Video Adjustments: Fine-tune brightness, contrast, hue, saturation, and orientation on the fly during playback.
  • Smart Playback Memory: Automatically remembers playback progress for videos and tracks recent media history.
XAudio2 & Built-in DSP

Advanced Audio & DSP Routing

  • Simultaneous Multi-Device Output: Route audio to multiple playback devices (wired headphones, Bluetooth earbuds, external speakers) at the same time with independent per-device volume controls.
  • Built-in Digital Reverb: Apply studio reverb effects directly to audio streams with zero external DSP dependencies.
