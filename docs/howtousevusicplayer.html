Getting Started & User Guide

Thank you for downloading Vusic Player! Powered by Flyleaf, FFmpeg, SoX DSP, and XAudio2, Vusic Player is built for simultaneous multi-device audio routing, real-time audio & video effects, and integrated subtitle & lyric editing. Explore the guides below to get started.

1. Multi-Device Audio Output

Play audio simultaneously across multiple speakers, Bluetooth headphones, and virtual audio devices at the exact same time—with independent volume control for every connected output.

How It Works

Stream to Multiple Audio Endpoints Simultaneously

Share music with someone on a second pair of headphones or output to both desk speakers and wireless earbuds without Windows audio switching.

  1. 1 Open the Multi-Device Output panel from the player controls and toggle Multi-Device Output on.
  2. 2 Vusic Player automatically detects all active playback endpoints (e.g., Speakers, Bluetooth Headphones, or FxSound) and lists them under Devices.
  3. 3 Drag each device's individual Volume Slider (0%–100%) to balance audio levels independently per output.
Refresh & Search Devices: Plugged in a new headset mid-song? Click the Refresh icon next to Devices to re-scan endpoints, or use the Search icon to filter long device lists.
List Mode View: Toggle List Mode at the top of the panel for a compact view when managing multiple connected outputs.
Hot-Swap Safe: If a Bluetooth device disconnects during playback, Vusic Player automatically detaches that endpoint while keeping audio playing smoothly on your remaining devices.

2. Using Audio Reverb

Add spacious room acoustics, hall ambience, or cathedral depth to your audio tracks—either live while a song is playing or across an entire folder of audio files at once.

Method 1 • Now Playing

Apply Reverb While Audio is Playing

Preview and apply reverb directly to your active audio track from the player controls without leaving your library.

  1. 1 Start playing any audio track and open the Audio Reverb panel from the player toolbar.
  2. 2 Choose an acoustic preset under Style (such as Cathedral (Recommended)) and click Apply Reverb.
  3. 3 Check Show More Options to fine-tune individual acoustic parameters or switch output modes:
Output Mode (Memory Stream vs. Export to File): Stream the reverb effect instantaneously in memory without creating extra files, or enable Export to File to save a processed copy to disk.
Acoustic Sliders: Customize Reverberance %, HF Damping %, Room Scale %, and Stereo Depth % to dial in your exact sound.
Supported Media: Audio Reverb is currently supported exclusively for audio files.
Live Audio Reverb panel with expanded acoustic controls
Method 2 • Batch Processing

Apply Reverb to Multiple Audio Files at Once

Need to convert an entire playlist or album? Click Apply Reverb to Multiple Files inside the Audio Reverb panel to open the dedicated batch processor.

1

Add Your Tracks

Click + Add Audio Files to queue one or multiple songs. Vusic Player processes all added files in parallel across your CPU cores for fast conversion.

2

Configure Reverb Options

Select a Style preset or customize Reverberance %, HF Damping %, Room Scale %, Stereo Depth %, Pre-Delay (ms), and Wet Gain (dB).

3

Choose Output & Apply

Save all tracks to your Default Music Folder, pick a shared export folder, or check Custom Directories for individual audio files, then click Apply Reverb.

3. Using Pitch Control

Shift playback pitch smoothly in real time while watching videos or listening to music, or batch-export pitch-shifted audio tracks using Rubberband DSP.

Method 1 • Live Audio & Video Playback

Adjust Pitch While Audio or Video is Playing

Fine-tune the key or tone of any playing song or video on the fly without altering playback speed.

  1. 1 Start playing any audio or video file and open the Pitch Control popup from the player controls.
  2. 2 Drag the Audio Pitch slider left or right to raise or lower the pitch instantaneously (default value is 1).
  3. 3 Click Reset at any moment to snap back to the original pitch (1).
Live Pitch Control popup (works for both audio and video)
Method 2 • Batch Processing Audio Files Only

Apply Pitch Change to Multiple Audio Files at Once

Click Apply Pitch To Multiple Audio Files inside the Pitch Control popup to batch-export pitch-shifted audio tracks. (Note: Multi-file batch pitch processing is available for audio files only.)

1

Add Audio Files

Click + Add Audio Files to queue one or more songs for parallel multi-core pitch shifting.

2

Set Pitch, Semitones, or Octaves

Enter your desired shift in Pitch Shift (ratio), Semitones Shift, or Octaves Shift—all three fields automatically stay in sync.

3

Export Processed Tracks

Choose your Export Directory (or check Custom Directories for individual audio files) and click Apply Pitch Change.

4. Using the Subtitle Editor

Create new subtitle tracks from scratch, load external subtitle files, or extract embedded subtitle tracks directly from your playing video.

Built-in Video Tool

Edit Embedded Tracks or Author New Subtitle Cues

Manage subtitle cues visually with duration controls or switch straight to raw text editing without leaving Vusic Player[cite: 1].

1

Load or Create a File

Check Subtitle(s) from video and pick an embedded track via Select Subtitle Track, or click + New Subtitle File / Load Subtitle File[cite: 1].

2

Edit Cues & Duration

Select any cue from the right-hand list to modify its text, adjust Subtitle Duration (seconds), or use Insert New Subtitle Cue Below / Above[cite: 1]. Toggle Text Editor for raw editing[cite: 1].

3

Save & Apply

Use Save File or Save As to export your subtitle file to disk, or click Save and Close to apply your edits immediately to playback[cite: 1].

Synchronized LRC & Live Lyrics Live Sync & Cue Authoring

5. Using the Lyrics Editor

Author timestamped lyric files, import existing .lrc files, or pull active track lyrics straight into the editor for timing adjustments.

Built-in Audio Tool

Build and Fine-Tune Synchronized Song Lyrics

Load currently playing lyrics with a single click or build line-by-line timed lyric cues for live playback display[cite: 2].

1

Import or Start Fresh

Click Load Current Lyrics to pull in lyrics from the active song, open an existing file via Load Lyric File, or click + New Lyric File[cite: 2].

2

Arrange Lyric Cues

Click any line under Lyrics to edit its text, set Lyric Cue Duration (seconds), or add lines using Insert New Lyric Cue Below / Above[cite: 2]. Switch to Text Editor anytime[cite: 2].

3

Save to Track

Export your .lrc file using Save File or Save As, or click Save and Close to update the live lyrics display immediately[cite: 2].
